# Caster Curios Bonus 灿奇术饰

**Caster Curios Bonus** introduces a variety of curios accessories for **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks**. These powerful or interesting items can be found in loot chests throughout the world and offer a wide range of effects, from boosting spell power to manipulating cooldowns and health. Currently most curios are inspired by **ARAM: Mayhem (海克斯大乱斗 in CN)** of LOL.

## Features

### Eureka
**Effect:** Gain cooldown reduction based on your extra spell power across all schools.  
- Automatically scales with your spell attributes.
- Ideal for spellcasters who want to cast more frequently.

### Giant Killer
**Effect:** Deal more spell damage to creatures larger than yourself.  
- Damage scales with the target's size relative to the player.
- Maximum damage boost is capped at 2× normal damage.

### Mana Overflow
**Effect:** Consume more mana, but your spells deal and heal for more.  
- Increases spell damage, healing, and spell level.
- Great for high-risk, high-reward playstyles.

### Mind to Matter
**Effect:** Gain extra maximum health based on your maximum mana.  
- Transforms your mana pool into additional survivability.

### All by Movement
**Effect:** Blink-type spells have their cooldowns halved.  
- Perfect for mobility-focused builds.

### Night Hunter
**Effect:** Gain true invisibility and speed after killing an enemy.  
- Ideal for assassins or stealthy spellcasters.

### Crimson Pledge
**Effect:** Your spell lifesteal also heals nearby allies.  
- Supports cooperative play and team healing strategies.

### Venomous Assault
**Effect:** Your non-Nature spells detonate Poison on the target, dealing its remaining damage instantly.  
- Turns enemy poison effects into a powerful burst of additional damage.

## Configuration

All curios and their effects can be customized in the configuration file: **config/CasterCuriosBonusConfig.toml**

- Adjust multipliers, scaling factors, and effect durations.
- Add support for extra spell schools or tweak how each item interacts with players.

## Notes

- Curios are found in loot chests throughout dungeons and other generated structures (e.g. Vanilla, IDAS, betterdeserttemples).
- Make sure to check the configuration file for advanced customization.

Enhance your spellcasting adventure and explore new magical synergies with **Caster Curios Bonus**!
